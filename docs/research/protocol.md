# Reasoning after AI assistance

## Study question and estimand

A person's improved score while viewing an answer could reflect copying, insight, or both. This protocol asks whether a structured interaction during training changes **subsequent unaided performance** on new items. The unit of assignment and primary analysis is the participant. The primary estimand is the mean difference in immediate, unaided transfer accuracy between assignment to metacognitive assistance and assignment to conventional assistance, among eligible randomized participants in the approved study population.

All three conditions commit an independent answer, rationale and confidence before assistance. That common gate allows decisions to be compared before and after assistance. It also means this design cannot identify the effect of requiring an initial answer itself. Testing that separate question would require a different, ethics-reviewed timing manipulation, for example independent-first versus immediate access, with a common unaided post-test and without treating a control group's post-exposure answer as an independent baseline.

## Questions and hypotheses for preregistration

| Question | Directional prediction | Measure and comparator |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Does prompting people to articulate uncertainty and consider counterevidence improve transfer? | H1, primary. Higher average post-test accuracy. | Unaided performance on held-out items, metacognitive versus conventional assistance, adjusting for baseline. |
| Does explicitly evaluating AI output improve calibration? | H2, secondary. Lower average post-test Brier score. | Confidence in correctness on objectively keyed items, structured reflection versus conventional. |
| Does assistance change evaluation of recommendations? | Exploratory. Examine separately when the recommendation is correct or incorrect. | Among initial disagreements with a scorable recommendation that was seen, compare the proportion of final choices adopting it. Report available opportunities by group. |
| Do any differences persist? | Exploratory delayed effect. | Unaided accuracy at the prespecified follow-up, with attrition by group. |

## Draft protocol

1. **Review and freeze.** The investigator and academic supervisor specify eligibility, recruitment channels, compensation, accessibility accommodations, languages, duration, withdrawal deadline, provider terms, escalation contacts and retention in the study record. Obtain institutional ethics clearance before recruitment. Pilot item wording, parallel-form difficulty, instructions, exposure logs and time estimates with the appropriate approval. Preregister the primary contrast, available sample, exclusions and analysis before outcome inspection.
2. **Consent.** Show the approved versioned information sheet, explain AI involvement and withdrawal, and record affirmative agreement with time and form version. Declining exits without research enrollment. A practice account and research consent are distinct. Issue a random study code. Store the restricted identity link separately. Never send it to the model.
3. **Baseline.** Complete one parallel form unaided, including confidence. Assign baseline form A or B at enrollment independently of treatment. Score against frozen keys. Set the policy for incomplete baseline before randomization. Use the other form at immediate post-test. Reserve separate training and follow-up items to reduce memorization. Balance order within arms and record the form sequence.
4. **Assignment.** After baseline, allocate centrally once per enrollment to conventional, metacognitive, or structured reflection at the approved allocation ratio. For a 1:1:1 design, use concealed, randomly permuted blocks within form sequence, with varying block sizes. Keep the allocation sequence server-side. Handle concurrent requests and resumed sessions by returning the original assignment. Participants see the assistance they receive. Mask outcome scorers and analysts to labels where feasible.
5. **Training.** Present comparable tasks drawn from a frozen set spanning logic, evidence quality, probability, decision-making under uncertainty, critical thinking, alternative hypotheses and contradictory evidence. Commit the initial answer, explanation, confidence and server time before AI is available. In conventional assistance, offer the answer and explanation on request. In metacognitive assistance, elicit a specific uncertainty before showing the answer and counterevidence. In structured reflection, show the answer and require the participant to identify supporting evidence, a weakness and an alternative before revision. Record whether advice was displayed. Keep provider, model version, common recommendation, decoding settings, item, incentives and display conventions matched wherever possible. If cached, versioned, expert-reviewed responses are used, disclose that choice. Differences in reading time and mandatory prompts are part of the assigned packages.
6. **Revision and reflection.** Collect final choice, confidence and a short reflection on the evidence that mattered or could change the conclusion. Include an explicit skip/stop route. A follow-up prompt can ask about a claim the participant made. Score only prespecified item outcomes or observable features of submitted reasoning.
7. **Transfer and debrief.** Administer the held-out post-test without AI and before item feedback. Optionally administer a distinct unaided delayed form in a prespecified window. Debrief, explain possible AI errors and provide withdrawal contacts. Report follow-up coverage as well as performance.
8. **Withdrawal and retention.** Stop new collection upon withdrawal. Verify the request against the restricted link, apply the approved deletion policy to responses, events and revocable exports, and record fulfillment without retaining response content. Set an approved retention period and destruction date for identity links, raw responses, exports and backups. Explain any limits after irreversible anonymization in consent. Audit the withdrawal and expiry workflows with test accounts before recruitment.

## Validity checks

Record model/prompt revisions, provider outages, missing responses, unequal exposure and item revisions. Prespecify how to handle interruptions and late follow-ups. Retain assignment for the primary comparison even when assistance was skipped. An agreement with AI reveals agreement on an observed choice. It cannot establish why a participant made that choice. Analyze text only under the approved rubric and use aggregated reporting with disclosure review.
